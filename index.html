Why The Fix

Rare micro-lots from the Cordillera to Mindanao

Each month we cup dozens of samples from smallholder farms across Luzon and Mindanao and choose the one lot that stopped us mid-sip. Most are smaller than 300 kg. Once they're gone, they're gone.

  • One exceptional lot a month. A 250 g bag, whole bean or ground for your brewer.
  • Roasted to order. Small batches on Padre Faura, shipped within 48 hours of roasting.
  • The story in every box. An origin card with the farm, tasting notes, and a tested brew recipe.

gear

Sourcing

Making an impact at origin and at home

We buy directly from the same farms year after year, pay above the farm-gate price, and put part of every subscription back into the places that grow it.

  • 1,600 masl

    Atok, Benguet

    Highland smallholders of Atok

    Family plots tucked between vegetable terraces. We pre-finance each harvest so growers aren't forced to sell cherry early at a loss.

    Harvest pre-financing

  • 1,500 masl

    Sagada, Mountain Province

    Shade growers of Sagada

    Arabica grown under pine and alnus trees. Part of every bag funds raised drying beds that cut mold losses in the rainy harvest months.

    Drying-bed fund

  • 1,350 masl

    Kapatagan, Davao del Sur

    Women-led processors of Mt. Apo

    A processing group on the slopes of Mt. Apo known for careful honey lots. We commit to buying their full honey lot every season.

    Multi-year buying commitment

At home in Ermita

Good for the block, too

  • Spent grounds go to community gardens around Manila every week.
  • Subscription boxes and cup carriers are recyclable paper, no plastic tape.
  • Every active subscription plants one tree a month with reforestation partners in the Cordillera.
