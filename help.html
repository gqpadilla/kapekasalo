FAQs

Good to know

How does ordering online work?+
Add drinks and pastries to your bag, check out, then pay by GCash QR or choose to pay at pickup. We start making your order once it is paid.
Where do I pick up?+
At our café on Padre Faura St., Ermita, Manila, 1000 Metro Manila. Show your order number (like KK-0012) at the counter. Pickup is always free; we don't deliver yet.
How do I pay with GCash?+
After checkout you'll see our QR code. Scan it with GCash, Maya or any bank app, send the exact amount, then type the reference number from your payment receipt. We check it and mark your order paid.
What if my GCash payment isn't confirmed?+
Make sure you typed the reference number exactly as shown in your app. If we can't match it, your order page tells you why and lets you send it again. You can also email us.
Can I change or cancel my order?+
Unpaid orders can be cancelled from your account. Once an order is paid, email us or tell the counter right away and we'll sort it out.
What is the Kasalo Bundle?+
Pair any drink with a pastry or rice cake and save on each pair. The discount is applied automatically in your bag.
Do you give the Senior Citizen and PWD discount?+
Yes, 20% off at the counter. Please show your OSCA or PWD ID when you order.
How do points work?+
Log in when you order and you earn 1 point for every ₱50 you spend. Your points show on your account page.
What are your hours?+
Monday to Friday 6AM – 10PM, Saturday and Sunday 7AM – 10PM.
I have an allergy. What's in your food?+
Many of our rice cakes and pastries use coconut, milk, eggs, wheat or nuts. Ask the counter or email us before you order and we'll tell you what's in each item.

Still stuck? Email kasalokape@gmail.com.

Privacy policy

Your information

We collect only what we need to run your orders: your name, email address, your orders, and the GCash reference number you send us. If you create an account we also keep your points and any The Fix subscription. If you join our newsletter we keep your email address.

We use this to make and hand over your order, send receipts and account emails, answer your questions, and, if you joined the newsletter, tell you about new drinks. We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Your data is stored with our database provider, Supabase. Only Kasalo Kape staff can see orders, and every change they make is logged.

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) you can ask to see, correct or delete your information. Email kasalokape@gmail.com and we'll reply within a few days.

Terms of service

The fine print

Prices are in Philippine pesos and may change. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay.

An order is confirmed once it is paid. Online orders are for pickup at our café only. Please pick up within the day; drinks are best fresh.

If something is wrong with your order, tell us at the counter or by email and we'll make it right, by remaking it or refunding you. GCash refunds are sent back to the number that paid.

The Fix subscriptions renew on the schedule you chose until you pause or cancel them from your account.

Accessibility

Made for everyone

We want everyone to be able to order from Kasalo Kape. This site is built to work with a keyboard and screen readers, with clear labels, visible focus outlines, readable color contrast, and reduced motion when your device asks for it.

If something on the site is hard to use, please tell us at kasalokape@gmail.com and we'll fix it. You can always order at the counter too.
