        About Us

        What's a kasalo?

        ka- (companion) + salo (to share a meal). A kasalo is the person you share a table with, someone who eats from the same plate.

        Kasalo Kape is our little table on Padre Faura: Filipino coffee, coolers, and rice cakes, priced for every day and made to be shared.
